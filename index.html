wildlife and conservation

.Preserving Biodiversity: Maintaining healthy animal and plant populations to keep natural food webs and ecological functions stable.
.Habitat Protection: Safeguarding forests, wetlands, and rangelands so species can feed, migrate, and reproduce.

our wildlife heritage

More than 60% to 70% of Kenya's wildlife lives outside formal state-protected areas on community and private lands.
Iconic species include African elephants (such as those in Tsavo and Meru National Parks), lions, black rhinos, hirola, sable antelope, and giraffes facing localized threats.
